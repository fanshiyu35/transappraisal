Where the module is in use — public-sector case summaries

Customer names are withheld under our customer-confidentiality commitments. The summaries below describe the kinds of public-sector organisations using the Staged-Delay BCA module in production.

Regional transport authority

Multi-stage rail investment programme, Netherlands

A regional transport authority models its rail investment programme as a chain of stages and uses the module to report how slippage in early stages propagates to net present value and the benefit-cost ratio.

National railway planning office

Corridor upgrade appraisal, Scandinavia

A national railway planning office uses stage dependency graphs to appraise phased corridor upgrades, with interaction terms between stages reported separately.

Urban transport authority

Metro extension business case, Germany

An urban transport authority runs SDI-BCA on a staged metro extension, using the marginal decomposition to identify which stage carries the largest delay risk.

Provincial road authority

Road investment programme, Belgium

A provincial road authority applies staged-delay costing across its multi-year programme before submission to the regional budget process.

Metropolitan planning agency

Transit priority scheme appraisal, United Kingdom

A metropolitan planning agency appraises a transit priority scheme with fractional-year pro-rating of delays across construction, commissioning and operations stages.

Public transport operator

Fleet and infrastructure staging, Austria

A public transport operator uses the module to compare staging sequences for a joint fleet and infrastructure investment, reporting the benefit-cost ratio under each delay scenario.

The figures above are drawn from licence administration records as of 30 September 2026. Individual customer names are not published; enablement records are available to auditors under appropriate confidentiality arrangements.
