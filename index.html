AppraisalStudio 4.2 · Staged-Delay BCA module

Appraisal that accounts for the delays that arrive in stages.

AppraisalStudio is our platform for transit and road investment appraisal. Version 4.2 introduced the Staged-Delay BCA (SDI-BCA) module — a licensed implementation of Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis that reports, stage by stage, how implementation delays change net present value and the benefit-cost ratio.

What SDI-BCA adds to conventional appraisal

01

Stage dependency graphs

Stage dependency graphs are defined per project template; delays propagate automatically to dependent stages with fractional-year pro-rating.

02

Marginal decomposition

Outputs include the stage-level marginal decomposition of the change in net present value and the benefit-cost ratio, with interaction terms reported separately.

03

Licensed method

SDI-BCA is implemented under a software and technology licence from the University of California, Irvine and Ms. Yanxi Liu (Agreement UCI-OTA-2025-017, 20 June 2025), following the procedure of NTRP Research Report No. 231 (June 2025).

04

European frameworks

Version 4.2 adds new data import profiles for European appraisal frameworks, alongside usability improvements to the scenario comparison view.
