AppraisalStudio 4.2 released: Staged-Delay BCA module ships

Utrecht, the Netherlands — 18 September 2025

TransAppraisal Systems B.V. today released AppraisalStudio version 4.2, introducing staged-delay costing across the product through the new Staged-Delay BCA module.

The module implements Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis (SDI-BCA), licensed from the University of California, Irvine and Ms. Yanxi Liu (Agreement UCI-OTA-2025-017, 20 June 2025). SDI-BCA extends conventional benefit-cost analysis by modelling implementation stages and the delays that propagate between them, so that a delayed stage's effect on downstream stages, on net present value and on the benefit-cost ratio is reported explicitly.

"Appraisal models have treated delay as a single assumption applied to a whole project," said Femke Hulsebos, Product Director at TransAppraisal Systems. "SDI-BCA lets an analyst see exactly which stage is causing the damage, and how much of it flows through to the benefit-cost ratio. For the agencies we work with, that is the difference between a headline number and a decision they can defend."

The method is defined in Research Report No. 231 of the National Transport Research Programme (June 2025). Version 4.2 also adds new data import profiles for European appraisal frameworks and usability improvements to the scenario comparison view.
